Logging achievements is the heartbeat of Yay-me. Every time you capture a win — no matter how small — you build a permanent, private record that lives entirely in your browser. No account, no cloud sync, no data leaving your device. The act of typing and saving is itself the habit you're building.

## Adding a Win

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the input field">
    The text area is the first thing you see when you open Yay-me. Tap or click it to bring up your keyboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your achievement">
    Type what you accomplished. Be as specific as you like — up to 280 characters. The more detail you include, the more personalised your AI-generated celebration phrase and tags will be.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your win">
    Press the **Save** button or hit **Enter** to confirm. Your achievement is written to your browser's local storage instantly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Celebrate">
    The celebration modal opens immediately, complete with a GIF and a motivational phrase. Your win is already saved before the modal even appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Tag chips on each card are automatically generated by AI when you save — no manual tagging needed.
</Tip>

## Your Achievement List

After saving at least one win, your achievements appear as a reverse-chronological timeline below the input field. The most recent entry always sits at the top, so your latest win is front and centre.

Achievements are grouped by calendar date, making it easy to scan back through previous days at a glance. Each card in the list shows:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Achievement Text" icon="align-left">
    The full text of what you typed, exactly as you wrote it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Timestamp" icon="clock">
    The time you saved the achievement, displayed in your local timezone.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Category Tags" icon="tag">
    Up to 3 short tag chips — such as `trabalho`, `saúde`, or `família` — automatically assigned by AI after you save.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
  Tags may take a few seconds to appear after saving, as they are generated asynchronously by the AI in the background. If the AI is unavailable, the card simply shows no tags — the achievement itself is always saved immediately.
</Info>

## Deleting an Achievement

Yay-me uses a two-tap confirmation flow to prevent accidental deletions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the delete icon">
    On the achievement card you want to remove, tap the delete icon. The card enters a confirmation state inline — no pop-up dialog appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the deletion">
    Tap the confirmation prompt that appears on the card to permanently remove the achievement. Tapping anywhere else cancels the action and leaves the card untouched.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleted achievements cannot be recovered. If you want to keep a permanent record before deleting, use the **Export backup** button in the app footer to download a `.json` file of all your achievements first.
</Warning>
