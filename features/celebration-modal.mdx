The moment you save an achievement in Yay-me, the app throws a small party. A celebration modal appears instantly — before you've even lifted your finger — giving you an immediate hit of positive reinforcement for every win you log, big or small.

## What You See

The celebration modal is designed to feel delightful without getting in the way. Each time it appears, you'll find:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="🎉 Mandou bem!" icon="party-horn">
    A bold, celebratory title greets every single win the same way — because every win deserves it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Celebration GIF" icon="image">
    A random celebration GIF plays automatically, filling the modal with movement and colour.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Motivational Phrase" icon="sparkles">
    A fun phrase appears immediately and may upgrade to a personalised AI punchline about your specific win within seconds.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Continue Button" icon="circle-check">
    A clear call-to-action closes the modal and returns you to your achievement list.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The GIF

Every celebration pulls a fresh, random GIF rated PG, so you always get something upbeat and appropriate.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Online">
    Yay-me fetches a random celebration GIF from an online GIF service each time you save. Because it's random, you'll rarely see the same GIF twice.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Offline or Service Unavailable">
    If the GIF service can't be reached — whether you're offline, on a slow connection, or the service is temporarily down — Yay-me automatically falls back to a curated set of local celebration GIFs bundled with the app. The celebration never breaks, even without a network connection.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## The Phrase

The phrase experience is designed to feel instant and then get better.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Instant phrase">
    The moment the modal opens, a fun motivational phrase from a curated static list is already on screen. There's no loading state, no blank space, no waiting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI upgrade">
    In the background, Yay-me sends your achievement text to an AI model. When the response arrives — usually within a couple of seconds — the phrase in the modal smoothly updates to a personalised punchline written specifically about what you described.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Seamless transition">
    The upgrade happens with a subtle pulse animation. You won't see a spinner or a flash of blank content — the phrase simply becomes more personal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If the AI service is unavailable or takes longer than 8 seconds to respond, the phrase stays as the fun static version from the curated list. The celebration always works, with or without AI.
</Note>

<Tip>
  The personalised phrase picks up on specific details from what you typed — the activity, the object, the situation. The more concrete your win description, the funnier and more relevant the AI punchline will be.
</Tip>

## Closing the Modal

You can dismiss the celebration modal in any of three ways:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Continue Button" icon="hand-pointer">
    Tap the **Continuar** button at the bottom of the modal.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Escape Key" icon="keyboard">
    Press the **Escape** key on a keyboard to close the modal immediately.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tap Outside" icon="xmark">
    Tap or click anywhere on the dimmed background behind the modal.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
