Yay-me is a lightweight, privacy-first Progressive Web App that turns your daily micro-wins into moments worth celebrating. You type what you accomplished, press save, and a celebration modal pops up — complete with a fun GIF and a personalised AI-generated phrase that riffs on exactly what you wrote. Everything you log is stored directly on your device, with no accounts, no servers, and no tracking involved whatsoever.

## What you can do

Yay-me packs a focused set of features designed to make habit-building feel rewarding rather than like a chore:

- **Log achievements** — type any win, large or small, and save it in seconds.
- **Celebration modal** — every save triggers an instant modal with a random celebratory GIF and a motivational phrase.
- **AI-powered phrases** — after the modal opens, an AI-generated quip personalised to your specific win replaces the default phrase in the background, without blocking the experience.
- **Auto-tags** — the same AI call returns up to three category tags (e.g. `work`, `health`, `family`) that appear as chips on each achievement card.
- **GIF celebrations** — powered by the GIPHY API, with a curated local fallback library so celebrations still fire when you're offline.
- **Streak dashboard** — a circular progress ring shows your current consecutive-day streak alongside your all-time record and total achievement count.
- **Backup and restore** — export your entire history as a `.json` file and import it back at any time, with smart deduplication so reimporting the same file never creates duplicates.
- **Daily reminder notifications** — opt-in browser notifications nudge you once a day to log something, so the habit sticks.
- **Offline and PWA ready** — install Yay-me to your home screen and use it without an internet connection; core functionality works fully offline.

## Privacy by design

Yay-me was built from the ground up with one rule: your data never leaves your device. Every achievement you log — including its text, timestamp, and AI-generated tags — is written to your browser's `localStorage`. There is no backend database, no user account system, no analytics that tracks what you write, and no third-party service that receives your achievement content. The only external calls the app makes are to fetch a celebration GIF and to generate a personalised phrase, and neither of those calls includes any identifying information about you.

## How it works

The full flow from input to saved achievement looks like this:

1. You type a win into the input field and press **Save** (or **Enter**).
2. A unique ID and a timestamp are assigned to your achievement, and it is immediately written to `localStorage`.
3. The celebration modal opens at once — a GIF loads from GIPHY (or from the local fallback if you're offline) and a default phrase is shown straight away.
4. In the background, an AI call generates a personalised joke about your specific win and up to three category tags. When the response arrives, the phrase in the modal updates silently — if it doesn't arrive within 8 seconds or fails, the default phrase stays and no error is shown.
5. The modal closes when you tap the button or click outside it.
6. Your achievement appears at the top of the timeline, and the streak dashboard recalculates.

<Note>
  Because Yay-me uses browser `localStorage`, your achievements are tied to the specific browser and device you use. **Clearing your browser data, cache, or site storage will permanently delete your achievement history.** Use the **Export backup** button in the app footer to save a `.json` copy of your data before clearing anything.
</Note>
