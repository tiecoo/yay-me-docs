You can be up and running with Yay-me in under a minute. There's nothing to install, nothing to configure, and no account to create. All you need is a modern browser and something worth celebrating — which, if you're reading this, you probably already have.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Yay-me in your browser">
    Go to the Yay-me app in any modern browser — Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge are all supported on both desktop and mobile. The app loads instantly and is ready to use straight away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your first win and save it">
    Tap or click the text input field, type whatever you accomplished today — it can be anything, big or small — then press the **Save Achievement** button or hit **Enter** on your keyboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the celebration modal">
    A celebration modal opens immediately. A fun GIF appears alongside a congratulatory phrase. In a moment, the phrase updates to an AI-generated quip personalised to exactly what you wrote. Tap the close button or click anywhere outside the modal when you're done celebrating.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your achievement is saved and your streak begins">
    Your win is now stored in your browser and appears at the top of your achievement timeline. The streak dashboard becomes visible once you have your first achievement logged — come back tomorrow to keep the streak alive.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  For the best mobile experience — full-screen mode, home screen access, and offline support — install Yay-me as a Progressive Web App. See the [Install App](/pwa-install) guide to do it in two taps.
</Tip>

## What just happened?

When you pressed **Save**, Yay-me created an entry for your win — complete with a unique ID, the text you entered, and the exact time you saved it — and stored everything directly in your browser. Nothing was sent to a server or tied to an account. The celebration modal opened immediately using a built-in phrase and fetched a GIF in the background. At the same time, an AI service generated a personalised phrase and up to three category tags for your achievement; when that response arrived, the phrase in the modal updated automatically and the tags were saved alongside your achievement.

Once your first achievement exists, the streak dashboard appears at the top of the page. It shows a circular ring with your current consecutive-day streak, your all-time record, and your total achievement count — all calculated entirely from the data in your browser.

<Note>
  No account is needed — ever. All your data lives in your browser's local storage. If you switch browsers, use a private/incognito window, or clear your browser data, your achievements will not carry over. Use the **Export backup** button in the app footer to save a copy of your history as a `.json` file you can restore later.
</Note>
