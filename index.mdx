## Get up and running in four steps

You don't need to configure anything. Here's how the whole experience works from the moment you open Yay-me for the first time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Navigate to the Yay-me web app in any modern browser — Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge. No installation needed to get started.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your win">
    Tap the input field, write whatever you accomplished — big or small — and press **Save** or hit **Enter**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the celebration">
    A celebration modal opens instantly with a fun GIF and an AI-generated phrase that jokes about your specific win. Close it when you're ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See your streak grow">
    Your achievement is saved and appears in your timeline. Log something every day and watch your consistency streak climb on the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>
