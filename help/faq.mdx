You've got questions — here are quick, honest answers to the things people ask most about Yay-me, from how your data is kept private to how streaks are counted and what to expect when you're offline.

<Accordion title="Do I need an account to use Yay-me?">
  No account required — not even an email address. Yay-me is entirely local to your browser. There is no sign-up flow, no password to remember, and no server that knows you exist. You open the app, log your win, and celebrate. That's it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where is my data stored?">
  All of your achievements are saved in your browser's `localStorage` under the key `yay-me:achievements`. That data lives only on your device and never travels to any external server or database. Yay-me cannot read your achievements — and neither can anyone else.

  <Note>
    Because data is stored locally, it is tied to the specific browser and device you are using. Switching browsers or devices means starting fresh unless you export a backup first.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use Yay-me offline?">
  Yes. Once the app has loaded in your browser, all core features work without an internet connection. The Service Worker caches the app shell so you can open and use Yay-me even when you have no connectivity.

  When you are offline:
  - **GIFs** fall back automatically to a curated list of local celebration GIFs bundled with the app — you will always see something joyful.
  - **Celebration phrases** are drawn from the built-in static list rather than the AI service.
  - **Logging, viewing, and deleting achievements** work exactly as normal.

  <Tip>
    For the most reliable offline experience, install Yay-me as a PWA using your browser's "Add to Home Screen" or "Install app" option.
  </Tip>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Will my data survive if I close the browser?">
  Yes. Browser `localStorage` persists across sessions, meaning your achievements are still there the next time you open your browser, whether that is five minutes later or five months later. Your data stays put until you explicitly clear your browser's site data or delete individual achievements inside the app.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens to my data if I clear my browser cache?">
  Clearing your browser's site data, cookies, or cached storage permanently removes everything stored for Yay-me — including all your achievements and your streak history. This action cannot be undone.

  <Warning>
    Before clearing your browser data, use the **"Exportar backup"** button at the bottom of the app to download a `.json` backup file. You can re-import it later to restore your achievements.
  </Warning>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why is my streak broken even though I log wins regularly?">
  Streaks in Yay-me are counted in **calendar days** based on your device's local timezone — from midnight to midnight. A streak is a run of consecutive days, each with at least one logged achievement.

  A few things to know:
  - If you logged a win yesterday but haven't logged one yet today, your streak is still **alive**. Today's calendar day hasn't ended yet, so nothing is broken.
  - A streak only breaks after a **full calendar day passes with zero achievements**. For example, if you log nothing on a Tuesday at all (midnight to midnight), your streak resets on Wednesday.
  - Logging at 11:59 PM one night and again at 12:01 AM the next morning counts as **two separate consecutive days** — both days have a win, so the streak grows.

  <Tip>
    If your streak seems unexpectedly low, check that your device's clock and timezone are set correctly, as streak calculations rely on your local system time.
  </Tip>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why didn't I get a personalised AI phrase?">
  The personalised phrase is generated by an AI model on a server-side function. If the AI service is temporarily unavailable, takes longer than 8 seconds to respond, or encounters any error, Yay-me gracefully falls back to the static list of curated celebration phrases.

  This is intentional. **Your celebration is never blocked by AI availability.** The modal opens immediately with a phrase and a GIF — the AI phrase is a welcome bonus when it arrives, not a dependency. You will always be celebrated, regardless of what the AI is up to.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I edit an achievement after saving it?">
  Not in the current version. Editing existing achievements is not yet supported. If you need to correct a win, the workaround is to delete the existing achievement and re-add it with the right text.

  <Note>
    Deleting an achievement requires a two-tap confirmation inside the app to prevent accidental removals.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I sync my data across devices?">
  Not automatically. Because all data lives in your browser's local storage, there is no cloud sync between devices. To move your achievements to another device or browser:

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Export your backup">
      Tap the **"Exportar backup"** button in the app footer. A `.json` file will be downloaded to your device.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Transfer the file">
      Move the downloaded `.json` file to your other device (via email, AirDrop, cloud storage, USB — whatever works for you).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Import on the new device">
      Open Yay-me on your new device, tap **"Importar backup"**, and select the `.json` file. Your achievements will be merged in — duplicates are automatically skipped.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is the AI reading my achievements?">
  When you save an achievement, your achievement text is sent to the AI service **only** to generate the personalised celebration phrase and category tags. The text is not stored, logged, used for training, or shared with any third party for any other purpose. Once the phrase and tags are returned to your browser, the transaction is complete.

  If you are offline or the AI service is unavailable, your achievement text never leaves your device at all — the static phrase list is used instead.
</Accordion>
